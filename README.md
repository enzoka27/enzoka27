### About Me

## About Me

I'm an Analysis and Systems Development student focused on backend engineering and artificial intelligence. I’m interested in understanding systems beyond the surface, how they scale, how they break, and how to design them in a way that feels robust and intentional.

I build projects to turn theory into practice, aiming for clarity in architecture and efficiency in execution. I’m particularly drawn to backend structures, data flow, and the logic that drives intelligent systems.

Outside of development, I value experiences with strong narrative and artistic depth, especially in games, films, and music. I tend to gravitate toward things that challenge perception or leave a lasting impression.
